# Ideas and decisions (current, takes precedence)

This file is the current snapshot of Sebastian's design input as of 13 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin). It is newer than the three concept pages in this folder and **overrides them** wherever they disagree.

The pages (`01`, `02`, `03`) are inspiration and brainstorming only. They are not a specification and not final.

## Locked for now

- **No currency and no shop / purchase system.** Everything is built from materials, in the spirit of Factorio. Keep this simple. The currency recommendation on page 3 (hybrid of barter, surface sales, knowledge, and energy) is **superseded** and must not be treated as the plan.
- **Pixel conservation** like Sandustry: every excavated pixel stays in the world; mass is not deleted. Do not copy Sandustry's visual style.
- **Storytelling without text and without voice.** No dialogue boxes, no narrator.
- **Progress is read first from the image.** Numbers are only an addition: depth, a later "of 6,371 km" (or similar), percent, and — if used — cubic metres. The picture comes first.
- **Start as a child in a sandbox**, digging the first centimetres with a plastic shovel. Large construction machines can sit in the background as a silent promise.
- **Target playtime** is about 100 hours for the full journey to Earth's centre (6,371 km, 1:1 scale). Phase 1 is only about prototypes and feel, not about that full length.
- **Working title:** "6.371 km". Title candidate: "6.371 km left".
- **Fossils are a fixed special feature.** On top of that, surprise finds may appear (for example a sunken city / "Atlantis", or a Pompeii-like buried place). Some finds are only there to be explored and have no mechanical use.
- **Building is not only downward.** The player can also build sideways: chambers for machines and haulage next to the shaft. There can be one shaft or several. The way of working may change from layer to layer.

## Still open

- **Excavated cubic metres as a progress value:** undecided. Prototypes may include a quiet volume counter so it can be felt, but it is not a locked feature.
- **Engine:** still open. Engineering has suggested Godot 4 + C#. Alternatives: Rust / Bevy, or Unity. Do not lock this without Sebastian's approval.
- **View:** a side-on cross-section with a little depth (2D / 2.5D). Still open in the details.
- **How much of the shaft is fully simulated** versus an abstract shaft above a 100–300 m window at the working face: a core idea in the concept pages, but Phase 1 should treat it as a testable variant, not as a given.

When a later idea in `01`–`03` conflicts with this file, follow this file. When this file is silent, the pages may be used as inspiration.
