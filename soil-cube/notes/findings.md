# Soil cube look

The photo is one soil block. Grass is on top, and the two cut faces carry the roots and stones. Those three cuts are mapped onto a cube, then the cubes sit next to each other on the ground.

Rows run back from the front. Three sliders set how many cubes sit across, how many rows there are, and the gap between them. Drag orbits the group. The scroll wheel zooms.

The slice stack is gone.

Zoom scales width, height, and depth together, so a cube stays a cube. The corners of each face texture fade out, with a rough edge rather than a perfect curve, so the corners are not a sharp square. That is a cutout: at the corner you see a little of the background, not a solid round edge. The middles of the edges stay opaque so the faces still meet.

The top is softer than the photo, because that face was a shallow band and had to be stretched square. The photo never shows the back or the far side, so those faces repeat a cut.
